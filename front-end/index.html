Charts

Move your mouse over a bar or dot to see the exact value.

1. Average sugar by food group

What it shows: Spreads, Biscuits and Chocolate have the most sugar on average. Waters have none.

2. Average calories by food group

What it shows: Chocolate and Spreads have the most energy, because they are high in fat and sugar. Drinks and Waters are lowest.

3. Sugar distribution (histogram)

What it shows: Most products have less than 10 g sugar. A few products (mostly spreads) have a lot. This is called a right-skewed distribution.

4. Sugar vs calories (scatter plot)

What it shows: Each dot is one product.

5. Missing values per column

What it shows: Very few values are missing (3.5% at most), so the data is good enough to analyse. Missing values are skipped in averages, not replaced.

Descriptive statistics

Mean = average. Median = the middle value when sorted. If the mean is much bigger than the median, a few very high values are pulling the average up.
